# Rigid constraint gallery plan

Eight isolated, open test pads share a checkerboard ground and fixed inspection
camera. Each pad reserves a clear central interaction lane:

- Fixed: two opposed rolling spheres and a midpoint joint.
- Point: one passive post, two rolling spheres, and a point joint on sphere A.
- Hinge: a narrow post, vertical panel, and sphere impact lane.
- Slider: a horizontal rail, carriage, and sphere impact lane.
- Piston: a vertical pole, sideways flag, and transverse sphere impact lane.
- Generic / Generic Spring: passive and dynamic blocks with an impact sphere.
- Motor: chassis and four wheels on a larger drive pad.

These are physics test scenes, not enclosed rooms. Doors, windows, wall
elevations, ceiling coverage, and arrival openings are intentionally not part
of the functional contract.
